Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you log in with Google OAuth or sign up with email, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo (if provided). While using Santerra's financial suite, your selected currencies, loan parameters, installment schedules, and investment models are stored locally on your device for immediate access.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used solely to authenticate your account and persist your personal calculation preferences across sessions. We do not use your financial simulations for automated profiling or credit scoring.

3. Zero Advertisements & No Data Monetization

Santerra does not display third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, or sponsored recommendations. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or calculation data to financial institutions or data brokers.

4. Security & Encryption

All client communications with Google Identity Services are conducted over secure HTTPS connections. Session credentials stored in your browser are protected under strict origin policies.

5. Financial Calculations Disclaimer

All calculations provided by Santerra (including currency exchange rates, loan amortization, installment schedules, and investment growth models) are for informational and planning purposes only and do not constitute financial, legal, or investment advice.

6. Contact & Account Deletion

You can clear your stored sessions and calculation data at any time by signing out or clearing browser storage. If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, please reach out via our GitHub repository.
